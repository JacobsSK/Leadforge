LinkedIn Strategy

The system around the messages. Run this daily — volume × consistency is what fills the pipeline.

Profile optimization checklist
  • Headline sells the outcome, not the job title: “I build websites that get local businesses found on Google — live in 7 days.”
  • Banner image: before/after of a client site, or a simple “Your business, online in 7 days” graphic.
  • About section opens with the client's problem (invisible on Google) not your skills.
  • Featured section: 2–3 site screenshots + one short case-study post, even if it's your own demo builds.
  • Custom URL, professional photo, open profile ON so non-connections can message back.
  • Post 2–3× per week: quick wins, before/afters, “5 things your site is missing” — stay visible between touches.
Connection cadence (per weekday)
DAILY
15–20 connection requests — under LinkedIn's radar; space them morning/afternoon rather than one burst.
DAY 1
Connect with the short note (no pitch — curiosity only).
DAY 3
Value message — one specific, free observation about their online presence. No ask.
DAY 7
Offer — the direct pitch with a low-friction CTA (10-min call or free homepage mockup).
DAY 14
Break-up email — polite last touch; highest reply rate of the sequence.
SA TIP
WhatsApp beats email locally — if you have a number, send the WhatsApp opener after the Day 3 touch.
